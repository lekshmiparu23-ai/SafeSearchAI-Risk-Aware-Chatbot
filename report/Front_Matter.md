## ABSTRACT

SafeSearchAI is an innovative Android application designed to address the critical need for safety and transparency in AI-driven communication. As Large Language Models (LLMs) become ubiquitous, the risk of users encountering harmful or unreliable content increases. SafeSearchAI implements a unique **Multi-Stage AI Risk and Reliability Pipeline** that acts as an intelligent intermediary between the user and the AI model (Llama 3.1 8B). 

The system performs three primary functions: (1) **Intent-based Risk Classification**, categorizing queries into Safe, Sensitive, or Harmful levels; (2) **Policy-driven Response Generation**, ensuring harmful queries are met with standardized refusals; and (3) **5-Dimensional Reliability Auditing**, which evaluates the AI's response across Relevance, Completeness, Specificity, Clarity, and Consistency. 

Utilizing the Cerebras high-speed inference API and Firebase for secure data management, SafeSearchAI provides real-time, color-coded feedback to users. The project demonstrates a scalable approach to AI alignment and transparency, ensuring that users can interact with powerful AI models safely and with a clear understanding of information quality.
